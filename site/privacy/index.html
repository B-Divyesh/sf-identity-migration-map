Privacy for migration evidence

Identity records can reveal sensitive paths, owners, and services.

CLI inputs and reports

The CLI reads the local paths you declare.

It writes reports only to the output directory you choose.

The CLI works without an account or hosted service.

Browser sample

The browser demo processes pasted text in memory.

It does not save demo text or send it over the network.

License data

A restored license token and its last result stay in your browser storage.

License checks send only that token to the Sociobot billing service.

After a successful check, reloads wait at least 24 hours before checking again.

Payments and hosting

Sociobot hosts checkout, and Dodo is the merchant of record.

This site has no analytics, advertising trackers, or third-party fonts.

Hosting systems may keep short-lived request logs for security and reliability.

Privacy requests

Email privacy@sociobot.in to ask about access, correction, or deletion.
